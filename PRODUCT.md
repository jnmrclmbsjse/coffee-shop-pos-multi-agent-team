# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop administrators and counter staff. Staff use the POS workspace while
standing at a counter, often on a tablet, to complete time-sensitive daily
operations with little room for ambiguity or repeated input.

## Product Purpose

An internal point-of-sale, catalog, inventory-counting, and daily-operations
tool for UCM Coffee Studio. It keeps sales, cash, stock counts, and staff
attribution anchored to clear business workflows so one location can operate
reliably today and a second branch can be added without changing the product's
core mental model.

## Brand Personality

Calm, accountable, and direct. The interface should feel dependable during a
busy shift, with plain operational language and deliberate feedback.

## Anti-references

- Consumer ordering experiences that favor promotion over operational clarity.
- Generic admin templates applied to the touch-first staff workspace.
- Decorative dashboards, novelty controls, or motion that slows down a counter
  workflow.
- Error, warning, and success states distinguished by color alone.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current task and its consequences first.
2. Make recorded zeroes, missing information, and pending work unmistakably
   different.
3. Preserve familiar controls and consistent language across staff workflows.
4. Design for fast, accurate touch use under real counter conditions.
5. Explain irreversible or accountable actions before staff commit them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, programmatic active
navigation, useful labels and live feedback, 48px touch targets in the staff
workspace, reduced-motion support, and state cues that remain clear without
color perception.
