# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Counter and shift staff use the touch-first POS on a shared landscape tablet
during service. Owners and administrators use the back office to maintain the
catalog, inventory setup, staff roster, and reporting records.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio uses this internal system to take and settle orders, maintain
the menu, record manual inventory operations, and review shop performance. It
succeeds when routine counter work is quick and unambiguous while historical
money, stock, and attribution records remain trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and dependable. The interface should feel operational and
shop-specific, with concise language that staff can understand during a busy
shift.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer ordering storefront, a marketing site, or a generic
analytics template. Avoid decorative motion, novelty controls, dense desktop
forms on touch surfaces, and visual treatments that obscure status or monetary
meaning.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current task and its next action obvious.
- Use the shop's vocabulary and explain consequences at the point of change.
- Preserve one interaction vocabulary across staff and admin workflows.
- Make status, permissions, and immutable history visible rather than implied.
- Prefer legible operational density over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard expectations. Staff-facing controls have a
minimum 44 by 44 CSS pixel touch target, visible focus, semantic state, and
reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
