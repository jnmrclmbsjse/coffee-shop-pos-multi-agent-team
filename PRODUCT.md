# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop administrators and staff use the system during active shifts and
back-office review. Staff need fast, touch-friendly workflows for daily POS and
manual inventory tasks; administrators manage catalog, staff, inventory, and
reporting.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio is an internal point-of-sale, catalog, and manual inventory
system for one shop preparing for a second branch. It should make daily records
clear and dependable while preserving the audit trail for sales, stock counts,
and corrections.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and operational. The interface should feel trustworthy under shift
pressure, with concise language and restrained visual emphasis.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer coffee-shop marketing, decorative café motifs, generic SaaS
dashboards, and unfamiliar custom controls. Do not make archival or read-only
records look actionable.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current task and business-day context obvious.
- Prefer familiar controls and compact, scannable operational data.
- Make irreversible records and read-only surfaces unmistakable.
- Communicate status with text and structure, not color alone.
- Preserve one consistent staff and administrator design vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks, visible focus, reduced-motion
support, and text/background contrast appropriate for WCAG AA. Staff controls
must remain usable on landscape tablets with at least 44px touch targets, and
status must not depend on color alone.
