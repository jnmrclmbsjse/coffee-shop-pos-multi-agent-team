# UCM Coffee Studio Back Office

## Register

product

## Users

UCM Coffee Studio owners, administrators, and staff use this internal system
from the back office or service counter during active shifts. They need to
complete operational tasks quickly, accurately, and without losing context when
interrupted.

## Product Purpose

The product manages point-of-sale, catalog, and manual inventory-counting
workflows for a coffee shop preparing to add a second location. Success means
each role can complete its work with clear state, reliable validation, and
durable records while Catalog, Inventory, and Sales remain distinct.

Inventory is based on manual open and close counts. The product does not imply
live stock, automatic recipe depletion, offline operation, hardware
integration, or inter-branch logistics.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The interface should feel dependable under time
pressure, use direct language, and make state changes easy to verify.

## Anti-references

- Marketing dashboards with oversized metrics, promotional copy, or decorative
  visualization.
- Novelty admin controls, hidden labels, or interactions that require a
  tutorial.
- Decorative gradients, glass effects, illustrated empty states, excessive
  card nesting, and color-only status signals.
- Interfaces that imply live stock, automatic depletion, offline operation, or
  unsupported branch workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current task and its consequences in view.
2. Prefer familiar controls and explicit state over clever interactions.
3. Keep saved data safe when validation or server rules reject a change.
4. Explain recovery beside the problem, then summarize it at save time.
5. Preserve operational context across desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use visible labels and focus indicators, semantic
keyboard-friendly controls, minimum 44px touch targets, non-color status cues,
clear error associations, and reduced-motion support. Responsive layouts must
avoid page-level horizontal scrolling and turn dense tables into labeled
records on narrow screens.
