# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop administrators and staff use the product during daily operations. Administrators manage the catalog, inventory settings, staff, reporting, compensation, and order history. Staff use focused point-of-sale, business-day, counting, restock, and order-history workflows. The interface must support quick, accurate work in a live shop environment.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio is an internal point-of-sale and operations system for a single coffee shop preparing for a second branch. It records sales, supports manual opening and closing inventory counts, and gives administrators reliable catalog and reporting tools without implying a real-time stock ledger. Success means daily work is easy to complete, historical records remain trustworthy, and important states such as missing counts are never confused with recorded zeroes.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, dependable. The product should feel calm and workmanlike, with direct language and familiar controls that keep attention on the task.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer coffee-marketing site, a decorative analytics dashboard, or an experimental interface. Avoid novelty that competes with operational data, inconsistent control patterns, decorative motion, and visuals that imply unsupported live inventory or multi-branch capabilities.

## Design Principles

1. Make operational state explicit, especially where unavailable data and recorded zeroes mean different things.
2. Preserve one coherent interaction and component vocabulary across administrator and staff workflows.
3. Keep dense business information readable through clear hierarchy, labels, and responsive structure.
4. Prefer trustworthy, audit-friendly presentation over decoration or novelty.
5. Describe only capabilities and data the product actually supports.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, minimum 44px interactive targets, readable contrast, and text labels in addition to color for every meaningful state. Support narrow 390px screens, horizontal access to wide tables, screen-reader descriptions for ambiguous data states, and reduced-motion preferences.
