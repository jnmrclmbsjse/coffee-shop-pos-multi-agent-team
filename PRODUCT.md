# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop owners, administrators, and staff using an internal point-of-sale
and back-office system, often on a laptop or tablet in a bright shop
environment between operational tasks.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio provides a trustworthy workspace for running sales,
maintaining the catalog and inventory records, managing staff, and reviewing
trading-day performance. Success means each user can complete their
role-specific work quickly, understand the current operational state, and trust
that money and historical records remain accurate.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard theatrics, oversized metrics, glass effects,
gradients, nested card grids, novelty controls, ambiguous chart-only meaning,
and consumer-marketing patterns that distract from operational work.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current business context and status before secondary analysis.
2. Keep dense operational data legible in bright ambient light.
3. Pair every visual encoding with readable values.
4. Make exceptional, empty, and error states explicit and inspectable.
5. Keep review controls distinct from actions that change operational data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve clear keyboard focus, maintain at least 44px
touch targets, pair color with text for state meaning, provide text equivalents
for charts, support reduced motion, and retain usable responsive layouts for
laptop and tablet screens.
