# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop owners and administrators manage staff between operational tasks on a laptop or tablet in a brightly lit back office. Their primary job is to keep the cashier roster accurate with minimal interruption.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio POS provides a dependable back-office roster so future register activity can be attributed to the correct cashier. Success means an administrator can quickly find, add, rename, activate, or deactivate a staff member without losing existing roster identity.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, operational.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative type, visual effects, oversized cards, playful admin patterns, and unfamiliar controls. The interface should not introduce personnel data beyond a name and active status.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the roster fast to scan.
2. Use familiar controls and explicit text for every status.
3. Preserve roster identity through renaming and status changes.
4. Make empty, error, and no-results states useful.
5. Keep prototype-only controls visually separate from the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for body text and controls. Provide visible 3px focus rings, semantic table and dialog markup, keyboard support, live status feedback, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal page overflow.
