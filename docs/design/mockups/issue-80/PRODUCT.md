# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coffee-shop owners and administrators reviewing the back office on a laptop or tablet in a bright shop office, often between operational tasks.

## Product Purpose

UCM Coffee Studio Admin provides a trustworthy read-only view of the current trading day, historical sales, reconciliation, and base-product performance. Success means an owner can confirm the trading-day status, understand takings, inspect exceptions, and export a defined report without changing operational data.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard theatrics, oversized metrics, glass effects, gradients, nested card grids, novelty controls, and ambiguous chart-only meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Put the business date and trading status before secondary analysis.
2. Keep dense operational data legible in bright ambient light.
3. Pair every visual encoding with readable values.
4. Make exceptional states explicit and inspectable.
5. Keep read-only review controls distinct from production actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve clear keyboard focus, maintain 44px touch targets and 48px fields, provide text equivalents for charts, support reduced motion, and make state meaning independent of color.
